# Domain glossary

This glossary is the consultable form of the ubiquitous language defined in
[chapter 01](01-linguaggio-ubiquo.md). It does not replace it: chapter 01 establishes **how**
vocabulary is governed, this chapter lists **what** it contains.

## How to read it

The **Nature** column declares the level of the definition according to the precedence rule of
`DM-01`:

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **N** | **Normative**: defined by an Italian normative or administrative act. The project cannot narrow it, broaden it or rename it |
| **G** | **National guide**: defined by an Italian implementation guide; prevails over the generic model |
| **S** | **Standard**: defined by the adopted international standard; structure, not clinical meaning |
| **P** | **Project**: operative definition of this area, permitted because higher levels are silent |

The **Reference** column indicates where the term is treated: `01`-`08` are chapters of this area,
`F01`-`F13` are modules of [`docs/10_fondamenti/`](../10_fondamenti/00-come-usare-questa-guida.md).

Entries of nature **N** report the source in brief form; full text is in module
[F02](../10_fondamenti/02-prestazioni-di-telemedicina.md) for services and module
[F03](../10_fondamenti/03-il-dato-clinico.md) for clinical data.

## Terms not to be used

These are not spelling errors: they are terms that, used in the model or in documentation,
introduce wrong meaning or a commitment the product cannot maintain.

| Do not use | Why | What to use instead |
|---|---|---|
| «Sessione» unqualified | Ambiguous between clinical act, media connection and application session | *encounter*, *media session*, *application session* |
| «Contact» in code | Collides with the element of contact details in the standard | `Encounter` |
| «Real-time monitoring» | Shifts regulatory classification (`D46`) | *collection deferred for periodic review by professional* |
| «Immutable audit» referring to versioning of entities | Versioning does not render immutable (`D42`) | *access log with fingerprint chain* |
| «Teleconsultation report» | Teleconsultation does not give rise to independent report | *collaborative report* |
| «Platform consent» | Does not exist: consents are five distinct objects | the specific type of consent |
| «Legal threshold» referring to technical parameters | No technical threshold is imposed by Italian regulations ([`V-12`](../11_registri/01-vincoli-in-vigore.md#v-12)) | *configurable product specification* |
| «Clinical quality» as claim | Has no verifiable definition (`D29`) | verifiable technical statements on resolution, continuity, outcome |
| «Automatic diagnosis», «clinical suggestion» | Out of scope ([`V2`](../11_registri/03-vincoli-fondanti.md#v2)) | *recording of professional's decision* |
| «The authenticated patient is identified» | Authentication and identification are distinct acts | *identification recorded by professional* |
| Names of companies, products, trade marks, domains of potential partners | Rule `R0`, prevailing over any other | *the integrator*, *a third-party EHR system* |

## Glossary maintenance

1. **An entry enters with its source**: level N, G or S with citation, or level P with the reason
   it is needed.
2. **An Italian entry without English translation is not an entry** (`D50`).
3. **Terms are not renamed, they are deprecated**: the entry remains, marked as deprecated, with
   the link to the new one and the date.
4. **Every new domain event passes through here.** The name of the event is a term of the
   language.
5. **Alignment with the national glossary is declared per entry**: coincides, specialises or is
   specific to the project. **[NV]** Alignment has not yet been performed; to be asked to the
   `COMP` area before freeze.
